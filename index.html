Prescription & Fitting Questions

Everything you need to know about prescriptions, pupillary distance, and materials.

You can upload a photo of your doctor's prescription during checkout, enter your SPH, CYL, Axis, and Add values manually, or email it to our opticians at rx@framelab.in. Our licensed optometry team reviews every prescription before surfacing.

Pupillary Distance (PD) is the distance in millimeters between the centers of your two pupils. It ensures the optical center of the lens is placed directly in front of your visual axis. Most doctor prescriptions include your PD (typically 58mm–68mm for adults). If unlisted, our digital card measurement guide can calculate it via photo with millimeter precision.

Japanese beta-titanium forged in Sabae is 48% lighter than stainless steel, completely nickel-free and hypoallergenic (preventing skin corrosion and green copper deposits), and features natural elastic shape memory that flexes around your temples without bending out of alignment.

We provide a 14-day hassle-free return window on all frames. If the fit, bridge width, or aesthetic does not feel completely natural, our concierge service arranges free doorstep pickup and full refunds or prescription adjustments.
